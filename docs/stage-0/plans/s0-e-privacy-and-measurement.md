## Kill-risk question

Can Hezo demonstrate, in a bounded synthetic proof, that a manual-route eligibility sentinel remains available without an account or optional contribution; security intelligence, anti-abuse, protection measurement, and optional product analytics remain non-joinable in access, storage, transit, logs, deletion, and backups; and one consented installation-month can be counted, withdrawn, reset, and re-consented without creating browsing history, a permanent installation profile, or a claim broader than Measured Monthly Protected Installations?

The answer is negative if optional consent changes the synthetic manual-route eligibility sentinel; a URL, domain, verdict, report, campaign, App Attest identity, stable installation value, MPD token, analytics value, IP, User-Agent, or cross-plane trace enters a prohibited plane or sink; any runtime role can read two sensitive planes; month rotation, current-month policy, idempotency, withdrawal, offline retry, reset, pepper destruction, or backup deletion fails; an analytics path exists without separate approval and consent; or the observed system cannot support the approved canonical definition and limitations without deceptive wording.

A negative answer pauses MPD, direct product analytics, related public audience-size claims, and Stage 1 entry under the current Stage 0 gate. It does not authorize disabling a future manual product path or joining data to repair the metric. Other independently authorized Stage 0 proofs may continue only within their own accepted boundaries; the complete Stage 0 exit remains blocked absent every required Pass or an Accepted rescope.

## Scope

### In scope

- One reviewed Stage 0 data inventory and flow model covering client state, edge/processors, four production-purpose planes, filter-runtime protocol state, restricted evidence, logs, replicas, queues, object stores, snapshots, backups, support, and deletion workers.
- Closed allow/deny schemas, role and network matrices, purpose-specific origins, keys, queues, logs, backups, retention jobs, traces, and administrative access for security intelligence, anti-abuse, and MPD, plus positive proof that the optional analytics plane has no runtime artifact under this plan.
- A minimal consent-state prototype with separate neutral choices, default-off behavior, accessible withdrawal, no product penalty, and no server-side consent profile.
- A synthetic manual-route eligibility sentinel proving only that account, URL Filter approval, MPD consent/state, and analytics absence are not prerequisites in the proof state machine. It does not submit a URL, calculate a verdict, or measure product quality/performance.
- The checked-in public synthetic MPD v1 vectors, plus reviewed proof-only fixtures for qualification, current-month presence, server-peppered deduplication, withdrawal, offline retry, reset, reinstall/restore behavior, re-consent, finalization, deletion, and backup restore.
- Proof that MPD measures a consenting production installation reporting protection enabled and healthy at least once in one UTC month, not browsing, blocks, people, accounts, hardware, continuous protection, or every protected installation.
- Proof-scoped edge, processor, log, metric, trace, crash, queue, support, export, deletion, and backup canaries with no live user or production data.
- Product analytics positively proved absent under P-012's current Proposed state: no target, origin, SDK, store, queue, schema, job, event, consent control, credential, or network request. Any affirmative analytics proof requires a material plan-and-fixture revision after separate Accepted scope.
- A preliminary proof-scoped privacy-label, privacy-manifest, processor-inventory, network-capture, US-first, consent, and methodology reconciliation. Final launch copy and claims remain owner decisions.
- Explicit teardown or separately authorized productionization closeout.

### Out of scope

- Selecting backend tooling, cloud, region, database product, KMS, queue, edge, processor, analytics vendor, crash SDK, anonymous-credential implementation, or final retention schedule.
- Accepting P-008, P-009, P-012, O-010, or O-011 by implication; approving public wording or a display location; claiming a final count.
- Proving unique people, Apple accounts, hardware devices, continuous protection, US residence, true installation retention, cryptographic unlinkability, manipulation resistance, or App Store compliance.
- Live URLs, browsing events, filter queries/results, threat data, reports, page content, real App Attest material, production receipts, provider data, user data, real IP geolocation, or captured production traffic.
- Cross-company advertising, attribution, tracking, IDFA, IDFV-based measurement, session replay, data-broker sharing, or a future cross-app ecosystem metric.
- Final O-017 report lifecycle or O-018 PIR protocol-state expiry decisions; the inventory verifies that open decisions remain visible and isolated.

### Assumptions and unknowns

- Four physically separated purpose planes can be operated without a common readable store, credential, trace, export, support view, or backup. Any cross-plane read or join falsifies this assumption.
- A random app-container secret can provide month-local idempotency without surviving the defined reset, uninstall, restore, or cross-app boundary. Its creation may occur before or after consent according to a separately frozen approved local-state contract; consent gates qualification, credential issuance, presence preparation/transmission, and network work, not necessarily inert local secret creation. The secret never leaves the device or affects route eligibility before consent. Survival, transmission, or reuse outside that scope falsifies the assumption.
- Edge and processor configuration can service synthetic requests without retaining IP, full User-Agent, bodies, tokens, or cross-plane IDs. A retained canary hit falsifies it; missing observer coverage makes the affected case Inconclusive.
- The Stage 0 proof can use an owner-approved proof-only retention and deletion schedule without accepting P-009's proposed production correction window. If a case needs an unapproved production duration, execution stops rather than treating 45 days as accepted.
- A subjectless capability can demonstrate stored-plane separation, but not cryptographic unlinkability. Any public manipulation-resistant or unlinkable count requires a separately approved standardized blinded credential, independent review, and production evidence.
- Product analytics is frozen to omitted for this plan because P-012 is Proposed. This plan tests absence and rejects attempted analytics scaffolding. A later owner-approved question and allowlist require a material revision that adds the complete consent, allowlist, deletion, processor, retention, and failure matrix before any affirmative prototype case can run.
- The minimal MPD body contains only schema, month, and token. It does not itself prove consent, production-build status, protection enablement, or health. S0-E can prove a bounded client qualification state machine and the absence of prohibited server fields, but it cannot claim that the server cryptographically verifies those local facts; any stronger integrity binding needs a separately accepted protocol and evidence.
- Documents 02 and 06 are not yet exact about whether the server digest key is a unique KMS-held `P_month` per month or a more generic measurement-plane key. This plan may not silently choose between them. The authoritative key hierarchy, month binding, rotation, destruction, and API wording must be reconciled and accepted before cases 049, 064, 068, or 082 execute.
- The checked-in withdrawal fixture calls its retry payload a `deletion_capability`, while the narrative/API direction also describes submitting the month token as a high-entropy capability. Its exact bytes, derivation, storage, wire representation, month binding, and destruction are unresolved. Cases 069–080 cannot execute until one closed contract and revised/confirmed fixture semantics remove that ambiguity.
- Evidence schema 2.0.0 gives each case one `environment_ref`. Class C cases span a physical device and approved isolated fixture services. Before execution, the evidence contract must either accept one truthful composite physical-device environment whose tool/version references cover both sides, or the plan/schema must split those observations into distinct stable cases; it may not hide a second environment in notes.
- The synthetic health adapter in S0-E is an input contract, not S0-B URL Filter health proof. The synthetic subjectless capability is a privacy-boundary contract, not S0-C App Attest validation or integrity proof. O-018 is inventoried only. None of those neighboring package outcomes can be claimed or inferred by S0-E.
- The manual-route eligibility sentinel is not a paste/share/QR implementation or evidence of verdict quality, latency, availability, or usability. The checked-in fixture assertion `protection_remains_available` maps only to this architectural sentinel in S0-E.
- The public evidence schema's contact fields represent prohibited boundary contacts, not privacy-canary values arriving through permitted fixture traffic. Privacy-canary insertions and prohibited-sink hits use a separately frozen bounded evidence vocabulary; schema contact arithmetic remains independent.
- The evidence schema has a root Inconclusive outcome but no per-case `inconclusive` value. The S0-A companion validator must freeze an executed-undecidable mapping and root precedence before execution.

## Environment and isolation

### Class A — offline reference

- Uses only reviewed public synthetic fixture bytes, closed pure state/policy functions, schemas, manifests, and sanitized metadata.
- Has no network, cloud credential, live target, processor, device identity, production data, or operational token.
- Validates governance, inventory, schemas, cryptographic vectors, contract mutations, definitions, analytics absence, wording limits, and evidence arithmetic.
- Cannot prove runtime access denial, edge logging, physical-device storage, network capture, deletion from a real store, or backup restore.

### Class B — isolated plane staging

- Uses separate proof-only security-intelligence, anti-abuse, and MPD stores with independent credentials, keys, queues, logs, backups, retention workers, and origins in an accepted isolated fixture network with no production peering, identity, credential, data, or live-threat access. Analytics has no target, origin, store, queue, role, key, log, backup, worker, or network path.
- Includes independently observed cross-plane route/access canaries, a proof edge/processor path, redacted observability sinks, synthetic measurement/withdrawal endpoints, proof-only snapshots/backups, and restoration into an isolated destination.
- Strips upstream IP/header/trace context before each plane and issues a new plane-local trace. Permitted fixture traffic and prohibited-route probes are separately counted.
- Uses only generated high-entropy privacy canaries and synthetic protocol data. Exact topology, processor configuration, credentials, runtime canary values, captures, and deletion receipts remain restricted.
- Resets every store, key, queue, log, observer, snapshot, trace, and processor state between runs; destroys all proof state at closeout.

### Class C — physical-device development proof

- Uses an accepted proof-only iPhone target with synthetic state, no production service, no live URL, no real App Attest material, no production entitlement dependency, and only the Class B fixture origins when connectivity is required.

- Exercises consent, qualification, withdrawal, offline retry, reinstall/restore/reset, and re-consent while keeping the synthetic manual-route eligibility sentinel independent through a synthetic health adapter; it implements no security request or verdict flow.
- Records OS/build/tool classes and opaque restricted device evidence without publishing team, bundle, App Group, device, account, installation, or signing identifiers.
- Deletes the proof app, local state, backups, fixture traffic, build output, and restricted raw evidence under the approved closeout.

## Privacy-canary accounting

Before execution, each applicable manifest freezes a synthetic canary family, insertion point, permitted sink set, prohibited sink set, expiry, and expected count. Runtime values are high entropy and restricted; public evidence uses only family IDs and bounded counts. Canaries cover URL/query/fragment-like values, security entity IDs, App Attest-like values, MPD-token-like values, analytics-batch-like values, IP/User-Agent/header values, deletion capabilities, credentials, and free text without copying real material.

Every observer receives a positive calibration through a separate approved calibration channel, resets to zero, observes the proof, and passes a post-run health check. A Pass requires every expected permitted sink hit to match its manifest and every prohibited sink hit to equal zero across edge, application, database, queue, object, trace, metric, crash, support, export, replica, snapshot, backup, and restored state. Missing observer coverage or ambiguous zero is Inconclusive; any confirmed prohibited hit is Stop and a privacy incident. These counts live in sanitized evidence referenced by cases because evidence schema contact fields have different semantics.

Closeout applicability depends on the pre-closeout execution path, not the eventual root decision, and the rule is frozen before the run. Case 900 is `required: true` only when regular-case execution already halted before closeout on Stop intent or an undecidable result; case 901 is `required: true` whenever cases 001–099 completed without that prior halt. Exactly one executes. A Stop or Inconclusive condition first discovered during case 901 remains in case 901: it triggers containment and teardown there, then derives the truthful terminal root outcome without retroactively requiring case 900. If either closeout cannot reach the schema-required complete state, the gate remains `not_decided` until closeout completes; it cannot finalize Pass, Stop, or Inconclusive. The unused branch is `not_applicable`, has no interval/duration, has inapplicable/all-zero contact counts, and cites the separate pre-run Accepted, unexpired closeout-applicability rule through the evidence schema's exception fields. The companion validator rejects both-required, neither-required, both-executed, path-mismatched, missing/expired applicability, retroactive branch switching, or fabricated intervals.

## Gates

### Pass gate

Pass requires all of the following together:

- every prerequisite is in its required current state; required Stage 0 inventory/definition records are current/approved, while the separate nonrequired O-010 and O-011 production-use records retain their exact owner-evidenced current nonapproved state (`not_started` or `pending`), accountable roles, and separate open limitations; P-009, final O-010/O-011, product analytics, and a public verified-count protocol remain unaccepted;
- all required cases and every manifest subvector produce the exact expected outcome with no unexplained skip, favorable aggregation, schema-only substitution, or material limitation;
- the synthetic manual-route eligibility sentinel remains eligible without an account, URL Filter approval, MPD consent, or analytics, and declining/withdrawing contribution creates no architectural route gate; no actual manual product, result-quality, availability, latency, or usability claim is made;
- every instantiated proof plane—security intelligence, anti-abuse, and MPD—has separate stores, credentials, keys, queues, logs, backups, retention workers, origins, traces, and access roles; analytics has no runtime artifact; no credential, route, tool, processor, export, or support view can read/join two purposes;
- every strict schema and nested-carrier mutation rejects prohibited fields, every prohibited route contact counter equals zero, every calibrated privacy-canary prohibited-sink count equals zero, and all permitted controls/observers are healthy;
- the full MPD vector, qualification, current-month, idempotency, pepper, withdrawal, offline retry, reset, reinstall/restore, re-consent, deletion, rollup, finalization, and backup-restore matrix passes under the approved proof policy;
- the monthly digest-key hierarchy and withdrawal deletion-capability semantics are reconciled across requirements, API direction, fixtures, storage, and evidence before their cases run; no ambiguous or generic placeholder is accepted after execution;
- retained measurement data cannot link across months or recover App Attest, analytics, browsing, user, account, device, IP, User-Agent, or security-entity identity; direct subjectless capabilities are not described as cryptographically unlinkable;
- qualification evidence is limited to the synthetic client state machine, Class C composite evidence is truthfully represented, and S0-E records no S0-B health, S0-C integrity, or O-018 expiry outcome;
- product analytics is positively absent across target, origin, SDK, store, queue, schema, job, event, consent, credential, request, binary, dependency, and processor inventories; every introduction mutation rejects, and no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, session-replay, or unreviewed crash SDK exists;
- preliminary manifests, processor inventory, App Store mapping, network capture, consent, methodology, US-first wording, limitations, and observed proof traffic reconcile without making a final launch/compliance/public-count claim;
- evidence identifies exact revisions, actual manifest IDs, environments, tool classes, cases, canary families/counts, policy versions, deviations, limitations, restricted references, expiry, and deletion; semantic validation, repository scan, and independent human public-safety review pass with zero findings;
- technical, iOS, backend, infrastructure/security, privacy, legal/product-copy, operations/deletion, QA, and final human reviews are complete and current; and
- case 901 records completed Teardown or separately Accepted productionization. Nothing remains active, readable, billable, scheduled, or retained by omission.

A Pass answers only the frozen S0-E kill-risk question in the approved synthetic environments. It does not authorize Stage 1, Stage 8, a production metric, a public audience-size claim, analytics, App Store submission, or compliance certification.

### Stop gate

Stop takes precedence over Inconclusive and Pass when any of the following occurs:

- the synthetic manual-route eligibility sentinel changes because MPD is declined, withdrawn, reset, or unavailable, or because analytics is absent; actual product/result/performance behavior remains outside this proof;
- any prohibited cross-plane read, route, trace, identifier, field, queue/object copy, backup restore, support view, export, or privacy-canary sink hit is confirmed;
- a URL, domain, query, fragment, verdict, report, campaign, App Attest key, MPD token, analytics value, IDFA, IDFV, raw IP, full User-Agent, stable installation ID, or equivalent enters an unapproved sink;
- consent is bundled, preselected, unclear, repeatedly nagged, recorded as a server profile, or work occurs before consent persistence or after withdrawal persistence;
- a token/vector/lifecycle/qualification/current-month/idempotency/withdrawal/offline/reset/re-consent/finalization/deletion/key-destruction/backup assertion fails under an otherwise healthy approved fixture;
- a proposed retention period, analytics system, public wording, anonymous-credential design, processor behavior, or owner decision is treated as accepted without authority, including conflating proof-scoped preliminary privacy material with final O-010 or narrow Stage 0 MPD definition/limitations approval with final O-011 wording/display;
- a real/captured URL, browsing/filter event, production token, App Attest item, device/account/application identifier, user data, live service, or production processor enters the proof;
- a privacy hard deadline is missed, a backup/restore resurrects readable raw state, an expired key remains usable, or containment/deletion cannot be verified;
- the approved plan, fixture, schema, environment, data flow, consent copy, processor, integrity, retention, or evidence revision drifts during execution; or
- evidence is false, corrupted, integrity-compromised, demonstrably non-reproducible because the execution record is untrustworthy, unsafe to retain, or crosses the public boundary. Temporary loss of access to otherwise intact, authenticated, policy-retained evidence is Inconclusive as defined below, not Stop.

Immediate containment is to stop contribution scheduling, confirm that no analytics scheduler or artifact exists, disable affected origins/queues, cut cross-plane access, revoke proof credentials/capabilities, isolate processors/stores/backups, rotate or destroy proof keys as authorized, delete affected synthetic data/copies, preserve only privacy-approved restricted incident evidence, and review disclosures and inventory. A Stop intent before closeout executes case 900; a Stop condition first discovered inside case 901 completes containment and teardown within case 901. A terminal `stop` decision is recorded only after required review and closeout complete. The proof does not authorize disabling any future manual product path; its eligibility sentinel remains independent unless a separately authorized safety response changes the proof state. Stop never counts as Pass.

### Inconclusive outcome

Inconclusive is available only after an authorized run with no confirmed authorization, privacy, consent, boundary, retention, or evidence-integrity failure when:

- an independently calibrated edge, sink, processor, backup, network, or device observer cannot establish that a zero is observed rather than absent telemetry;
- an approved synthetic fixture service, proof store, device, restore source, or external configuration is temporarily unavailable or confounded;
- a required immutable approved reference becomes unavailable midrun while independent evidence shows no executed bytes/policy/data-flow drift; or
- an authorized reviewer temporarily cannot access required restricted evidence while independent metadata shows it remains intact, authenticated, policy-retained, and uncompromised, with no falsehood, corruption, or leak.

The per-case outcome and bounded `observed_code` follow the preaccepted S0-A mapping. Missing approval, environment, manifest, processor contract, device authority, retention rule, or evidence location before execution leaves the plan Draft, not Inconclusive. Any confirmed leak, join, postwithdrawal collection, retention breach, or false evidence is Stop. Complete the already-applicable closeout case—case 900 after a prior operational halt, or case 901 when the undecidable condition is first discovered there—preserve the limitation, revise/reapprove, reset, and rerun every affected case.

## Evidence plan

The public bundle uses [evidence schema 2.0.0](../evidence-bundle.schema.json) and the exact accepted S0-A companion validator. It records root and per-executed-case intervals, expected/observed bounded codes, exact environment/revision/fixture/evidence references, typed contact counts, limitations, external states, reviews, closeout, and freshness. The companion validator proves case/prefix/reference integrity, real chronology, manifest binding, vector coverage, contact arithmetic, closeout applicability, decision equality/precedence, and leak safety.

Public-safe evidence may contain generic roles; reviewed public document/schema/fixture digests; bounded environment/tool/policy/version classes; case/outcome codes; aggregate permitted-request, prohibited-route, and privacy-canary counts; sanitized plane/field/access/deletion results; non-identifying rollup counts created only for the proof; limitations; review states; closeout; and non-enumerable opaque restricted references.

Restricted evidence outside Git contains device/account/application/signing/container identifiers; runtime secrets, tokens, peppers, keys, capabilities, canary values, network captures, IP/header values, endpoint/topology/processor configuration, access tests, database/queue/object/log/trace/crash/support/export contents, snapshots/backups/restores, deletion/key-destruction receipts, private approval records, raw evidence, and incident detail.

Public opaque references must not encode a person, provider, processor, device, account, application, project, environment, region, endpoint, store, token, date-correlatable run, or enumerable restricted identifier. A public digest is allowed only for reviewed public/high-entropy artifacts when it cannot confirm a restricted value.

The public bundle sets all eight schema privacy declarations to false: `contains_raw_url`, `contains_live_threat_data`, `contains_page_or_capture_content`, `contains_attestation_material`, `contains_credentials_or_tokens`, `contains_personal_owner_data`, `contains_private_approval_records`, and `contains_device_or_cloud_identifiers`. Raw working files, packet captures, proof stores, local device state, tokens/digests/peppers, queues, logs, replicas, snapshots, backups, restores, build output, and deletion receipts are removed under the proof policy. Public synthetic manifests remain only if independently reviewed and still governed.

## Limitations and unsupported claims

Even a future Pass does not establish that:

- one measured installation is one person, Apple account, household, hardware device, continuously protected device, or every protected installation;
- reinstalls, restores, migrations, resets, or seed loss cannot overcount, or background/offline behavior cannot undercount;
- a rotating pseudonymous token or retained App Attest record is anonymous;
- a direct subjectless capability is cryptographically unlinkable or manipulation-resistant;
- the minimal MPD request or persisted row independently proves consent, production-build status, URL Filter enablement/health, or absence of a kill switch/degraded state;
- the synthetic health adapter passes S0-B, the synthetic capability passes S0-C, or the O-018 inventory sets PIR protocol-state expiry;
- one evidence `environment_ref` proves two independently classified environments; Class C is only the specifically approved composite representation and must be split if reviewers find it lossy;
- the monthly server digest-key wording or withdrawal deletion-capability semantics were resolved unless the accepted reconciliation cited by the executed cases exists;
- a public verified count, one-million threshold, marketing wording, display location, or methodology publication is approved;
- P-009's 45-day direction, any final retention/backup period, O-010, O-011, O-017, O-018, or product analytics is accepted;
- true 30-day/90-day installation retention, geography, US residency, browsing/block volume, threat detection, or effectiveness can be measured from MPD;
- a production edge, processor, database, queue, KMS, backup, app target, SDK, privacy manifest, Privacy Nutrition Label, consent copy, privacy policy, rights workflow, or deletion system is selected or ready;
- manual paste, share, or QR checking is implemented, useful, available, performant, or produces a verdict; S0-E proves only the frozen route-eligibility sentinel;
- App Store, FTC, state privacy, COPPA, security, legal, processor, or advertising-claim compliance is certified;
- S0-A/S0-B/S0-C/S0-D/S0-F, Stage 0 exit, Stage 8, TestFlight, public beta, or launch gates passed.

Evidence expires at the approved review point and after any material client/storage, iOS, data-flow, schema, purpose, processor, edge, trace, consent, copy, qualification, token, integrity, retention, backup, analytics, methodology, or claim change.

## Teardown or productionization

Every run completes [the teardown checklist](../teardown-checklist.md) through case 900 or 901. Default for Stop, Inconclusive, or any run without separately Accepted productionization is Teardown.

Teardown first stops schedulers, issuance, uploads, retries, queues, processors, and metered work. It revokes proof identities, credentials, capabilities, signing access, and device registrations; destroys measurement secrets, peppers, keys, replay state, plane stores, queues, logs, traces, crashes, exports, replicas, snapshots, backups, restored copies, canaries, proof builds, device state, and temporary services under the approved rules; and verifies that no product or later proof depends on the spike. Only explicitly governed restricted result/closeout evidence and reviewed public synthetic fixtures may survive.

Productionize separately requires new stage-appropriate Accepted ADRs and an implementation plan covering supported app/service architecture, four-plane physical isolation, consent, contracts, integrity, operations, processors, observability, retention, deletion, backups, privacy manifests/labels/policy, incident response, migration, rollback, and independent review. Proof code, fixtures, tokens, identities, configuration, data, privileges, and shortcuts are not promoted. Stage 1 and Stage 8 remain subject to their own entry gates.
